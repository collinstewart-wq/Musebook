# Musebook
Musebook is an accessibility-first, humane AI project focused on helping people think and learn without surveillance or pressure. I’m looking for a Founding Engineer to help build a small, demo-ready MVP (4–6 weeks) so we can begin early pilots. If you enjoy building thoughtful tools and care about accessibility or education, I’d love to talk.

Musebook — Founding Engineer (equity-based partnership, no salary at start)

I’m building Musebook: an accessibility-first, humane AI tool to support learning, thinking, and creative work without surveillance or pressure. Our focus is people who are often underserved by mainstream tools — neurodivergent students, educators, and workers — and we design around calm, private, and permissioned interactions rather than tracking or gamified engagement.

What this is
- A founding role, not an internship, freelance gig, or casual volunteer position.
- Equity-based partnership: roughly 4–5% equity offered, with a standard vesting schedule (4 years, 12‑month cliff). There is no salary at the start; a small stipend or pay may be possible later with funding or revenue.
- MVP scope is intentionally small and focused: a 4–6 week, demo-ready build that shows the core idea and value to potential users and early partners.

Why this project exists (short)
- Learning and creative tools often assume a single “typical” user and optimize for attention/retention, not accessibility or dignity.
- Musebook exists to build a quieter, more humane assistant that helps people learn and create on their own terms — privacy-first, respectful of neurodiversity, and deliberately low-pressure.

What I’m hoping you care about
- Accessibility, inclusive education, humane tech, and building tools people can trust.
- Shipping useful, well-designed demos quickly and iterating based on real feedback.
- Clear communication, respect for people’s time, and ethical collaboration.

What I need from a Founding Engineer
- Take technical ownership of the 4–6 week MVP: implement the core demo, help shape the product decisions, and prepare a deployable showcase.
- Be comfortable working full‑stack or owning a clear technical slice (frontend + integrations or backend + model infra).
- Ship production-quality code, tests, and simple deployment/CI for the demo.
- Contribute to product thinking and future technical roadmap as a partner (not just an implementer).

Preferred experience (not strict)
- Early-career (1–5 years) or someone motivated by meaningful ownership and real-world impact.
- Strong fundamentals in web/frontend or backend development; familiarity with accessibility best practices.
- Experience or interest in privacy-preserving design, small ML/AI integrations, or education tools is a plus.

What this is not
- A short-term money play, contractor-only gig, or place for inflated equity negotiations.
- A role for folks who want immediate salary with no upside or for people who won’t commit to collaborative decision-making.
